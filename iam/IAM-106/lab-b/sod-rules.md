# Separation of Duties Rules: Prayan Bank Finance

These are the rules to check every line of the Finance access review against. A line that breaks a rule is a conflict, and the review must clear it.

## SOD-1

**Rule:** No one may be in both Finance Clerk Role and Payments Approver Role.

**Why:** One person could raise a payment and approve it alone.

## SOD-2

**Rule:** Only Finance staff may have Finance Ledger.

**Why:** Changes to the finance ledger must stay under Finance control, for financial reporting.

Built during SkillCube IAM training. All data is fictional (Prayan Bank).
